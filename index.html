Helpful information

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common questions before you make an enquiry.

Does CODEYSA-STP issue visas?

No. Visa decisions are made by the relevant government and immigration authorities. CODEYSA-STP can provide guidance and assistance with understanding documentation and application steps, but cannot guarantee approval.

Can I use the currency converter for an actual exchange?

Use it as an estimate for planning only. Banks, card providers and exchange services may apply different rates, fees and spreads. Always confirm the final rate before paying or exchanging money.

Can CODEYSA-STP help me explore business opportunities?

We can help you make an initial enquiry, understand local considerations and identify questions to investigate. Any investment decision should be based on independent due diligence and professional advice.

Is CODEYSA-STP part of FEHB Organization?

CODEYSA-STP is an independently managed organisation and an Official Strategic Affiliate of FEHB Organization. Both organisations retain separate identities, management and operational responsibilities.

Official strategic affiliate

Working with FEHB Organization

CODEYSA-STP is an independently managed organisation and an Official Strategic Affiliate of FEHB Organization. Our affiliation connects travel, relocation and business facilitation with wider community-development and international cooperation initiatives in São Tomé and Príncipe.

Through mutually approved activities, we support connections between visitors, entrepreneurs, investors, local communities and development partners. Each organisation retains its own identity, management and operational responsibilities.
