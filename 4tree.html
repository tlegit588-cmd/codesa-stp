Business development

Build a responsible distribution network

We welcome enquiries from agricultural retailers, farmer groups, cooperatives, agribusiness operators and potential regional partners interested in learning about 4Tree.

Who can enquire?

  • Agricultural input retailers and wholesalers
  • Farmer associations and cooperatives
  • Farm owners and commercial growers
  • Regional sales and logistics partners
  • Entrepreneurs exploring an agricultural distribution business

Before entering a distribution agreement

  • Confirm CODEYSA-STP's written authority and territory.
  • Obtain the official wholesale price list and margin structure.
  • Confirm minimum order quantities, payment terms and delivery arrangements.
  • Review product registration, import requirements and approved marketing claims.
  • Agree on training, after-sales support and customer complaint handling.

Request the latest official label and technical guide from the supplier. Do not guess the dilution rate, crop dosage or application frequency.

No. CODEYSA-STP does not promise profit or a fixed return. Any commercial proposal requires due diligence, written terms and independent professional advice.
